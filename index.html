What We Do

Four core activities

Our work runs from the first look at an opportunity through to the effective execution of an investment.

01.

Investment Opportunity Identification

Sourcing and identifying promising ventures, businesses and investment opportunities across African markets.

02.

Investment Evaluation

Assessing business models, financial performance, market potential, management teams, risks and investment viability.

03.

Transaction Structuring

Structuring investment terms, equity participation, financing arrangements, governance and transaction execution.

04.

Capital Deployment

Deploying venture, impact and growth capital into promising businesses and investment opportunities.
